What is a Non-Equi Join?
A Non-Equi Join is the exact opposite of an Equi-Join.


While an Equi-Join uses the equals operator (=) to find exact matches between tables, a Non-Equi Join uses other comparison operators—such as greater than (>), less than (<), not equal to (!= or <>), or BETWEEN—to combine tables based on a range or inequality condition


SELECT personal.name, professional.work 
FROM personal, professional 
WHERE personal.id < professional.id;

Understanding : 
Condition is ---WHERE personal.id < professional.id;



This means that MySQL compared each person’s ID with every ID in the professional table and checked where the personal ID is smaller (<) than the professional ID.
